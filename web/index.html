🐢 TamaPoke

A 386-Pokémon tamagotchi for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75. Install it from your browser — no Arduino, no drivers.

⚠️ Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

⚠️ Board not showing up? Read this first — it is almost always this.

A brand-new board does not appear as a serial port at all. The firmware it ships with never switches USB on, so your computer sees nothing and it looks like a dead board or a broken cable. It is neither. Put it in download mode:

hold BOOT → tap RESET → let go of BOOT

The two small buttons next to the USB-C socket. Nothing on screen will change — that is normal. Now press install and the port will be there. After TamaPoke is on it, this is never needed again.

Still nothing? Use a cable that carries data (a charge-only USB-C cable powers the board perfectly and carries nothing), and plug straight into your computer rather than through a hub.

1Flash the firmware

Plug the board in via USB and press install. Pick the port and wait. Takes about a minute. No drivers needed on any OS.

Browser not supported (use Chrome/Edge). Needs HTTPS.

🔄 Updating? Leave "Erase device" unchecked. The installer asks once, with the box already unchecked — click Next without ticking it and your creature, Pokédex and badges all survive, because the firmware is written around the save rather than over it. Tick that box and everything is gone, with no undo. The sprites on the microSD are untouched either way.

Installers before v2.7 erased it every time, without asking. They shipped one image covering the whole start of flash, the save included, and were configured to wipe the chip before writing. Both are fixed here. Even so, EXPORT on the serial console is the only real safety net: it prints your whole save as a block of IMPORT lines, and pasting that block back restores it. Worth doing before any update you care about.

A fresh board with nothing on it can be erased safely, and on a board that has never run TamaPoke it makes no difference either way.

LAN Battle v6 — No Phantom Moves (Beta)

🧪 Test build: both players must pick a move before the round resolves. Old move packets are rejected.

Both ESP32-S3 boards must use LAN protocol v6 to battle each other. The stable installer above stays unchanged.

Browser not supported (use Chrome/Edge). Needs HTTPS.

Leave “Erase device” unchecked. The beta writes firmware around NVS and FAT storage, just like the stable installer. Back up your save first if it matters. This build has not yet passed two-board gameplay tests.

🏆 New: REMATCH or EXIT after each LAN battle. Your Trainer Card shows wins, losses, win rate, and the last five opponents with their names and results.

Includes LAN protocol v6 phantom-action protection. Install this beta on both boards to battle each other.

Browser not supported (use Chrome/Edge). Needs HTTPS.

Optional: Custom OTA/app update (beta)

Already running TamaPoke? Start with Connect & inspect board. You can choose a local ESP32-S3 ota.bin or app.bin now or after the backup. Before any write, the updater checks the actual board's flash size, security settings, TamaPoke partition table and OTA-selected app slot. It then reads the whole 16 MB flash, saves a local backup and verifies that saved file byte-for-byte.

This works only with the exact 16 MB TamaPoke layout and an unencrypted ESP32-S3 whose OTA metadata unambiguously selects app0 at 0x10000. All other layouts, security configurations and OTA states are refused. A selected OTA slot does not conclusively prove which app is running if the bootloader fell back.

Connection trouble? Disconnect other serial apps or installer tabs, then hold BOOT → tap RESET → release BOOT and try again. This beta updater connects at a conservative 115200 baud.

2Load the sprites onto the microSD

  

    
Put a microSD into the board (it formats itself). Press Connect board,
       then a region. Any one of them works on its own — pick Johto or Hoenn if
       that is the game you want — and the others can be added later without
       redoing this one. Kanto is simply the usual place to start.

    
⏱️ This part is slow, and that is normal. The sprites go over
       the USB serial link, so Kanto's 40 MB takes roughly 10–15 minutes. Leave the
       tab open and the card in. The progress bar moves the whole time.

How to play

  
Care for your egg until it hatches, feed it, play, train it and complete them
     all. Tap the creature to pet it; swipe to open the Pokédex (sideways), the
     stat card (up) or set the clock (down); the physical PWR button turns the
     screen off (short press) or everything off (long press).
     More in the repository.
